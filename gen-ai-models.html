Artificial Intelligence, explained

Generative AI models create new possibilities.

Gen AI models learn patterns from large collections of data, then use those patterns to generate useful text, images, code, audio, video, and more.

What is a Gen AI model?

Unlike traditional systems that only classify or predict, generative models can produce original outputs in response to a prompt. They are becoming practical collaborators for writing, design, software development, research, and customer support.

✦

Language models

Large language models (LLMs) generate and understand natural language. They can summarize documents, answer questions, translate text, and help draft content or code.

▧

Image & video models

Diffusion and multimodal models turn text, images, or sketches into new visuals. They support creative exploration, marketing assets, prototyping, and visual storytelling.

⌁

Audio models

Audio models can synthesize speech, transcribe conversations, generate music, and transform voices—opening up new ways to make content more accessible and expressive.

✓
Use Gen AI thoughtfully

Outputs can be inaccurate, biased, or unsuitable for a specific context. Review important results, protect sensitive information, respect copyright, and keep people accountable for final decisions.
