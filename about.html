About this site

A friendly starting point for learning about Generative AI.

Our purpose

Gen AI Guide is a sample educational site that makes the building blocks of generative artificial intelligence easier to understand. It introduces different kinds of models, the creative tasks they can support, and the care needed to use them responsibly.

AI is moving quickly, but useful applications start with people who understand its strengths and limitations. We believe clear explanations are a great first step.
