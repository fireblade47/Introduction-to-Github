About this site

A friendly starting point for learning GitHub.

Our purpose

GitHub Guide is a sample educational site that helps new developers understand the essentials of GitHub. It introduces repositories, commits, branches, and pull requests—the everyday tools that make version control and teamwork easier.

Whether you are working alone or with a team, GitHub gives your projects a reliable home and a shared record of progress. We believe a clear first introduction makes collaboration much less intimidating.
